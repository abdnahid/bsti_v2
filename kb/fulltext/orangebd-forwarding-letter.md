To

Subject: Submission of Manage Service Proposal for BSTI e-Application Management System.

With reference to the development and implementation of the digital services of the Bangladesh
Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI), we hereby submit our Manage Service Proposal for the
required scope of work.

The project was initially undertaken under the Bangladesh Trade Facilitation (BTF) Project and
commenced on 12 January 2025, with an anticipated completion date of 31 July 2026. Subsequently,
the contractual arrangement was amended on 13 June 2025. Since then, we have continued to support
BSTI by providing service enhancement, expansion, maintenance, issue resolution, and technical
assistance to ensure continuity of the existing services. During this period, five (5) services have been
launched, with ongoing improvement and support based on operational and user requirements.

Following the anticipated completion of the project on 31 July 2026, the existing services, requirements,
and future development needs were reviewed and analyzed from 1 August 2026. Based on this
assessment, a detailed Scope of Work has been prepared.

Accordingly, the enclosed Financial Proposal has been prepared based on the defined scope,
considering the required manpower, technical activities, time, and resources for the enhancement,
expansion, maintenance, and technical support of the existing services.

We hereby submit the Financial Proposal for your kind consideration and necessary action.
