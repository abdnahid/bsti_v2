5.1 Why the Staff-Remuneration Basis Is High
 • The reference model carries a multidisciplinary team spanning management, business analysis, engineering,
      infrastructure, security, QA, training and operational support.
 • Many roles are costed for long person-month commitments across the rollout and support periods.
 • After development, the maintenance model still requires technical, support, training and helpdesk resources to
      sustain all 33 services.
 • The resulting reference cost is BDT 235,530,000 for development/rollout plus maintenance.

The service-based model provides BSTI with the complete commercial roadmap at BDT 186,905,000, while
the equivalent staff-remuneration basis is BDT 235,530,000. The proposed model therefore reduces the
reference cost by BDT 48,625,000, equivalent to an overall cost advantage of approximately 20.6%.
