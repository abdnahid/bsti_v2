       registered as a non-governmental organization bearing registration number 1713 (dated April 3, 2002 and valid
       thru April 2, 2027) under the laws of Bangladesh, and Orange Business Development Limited, a limited liability
       corporation registered under the laws of Bangladesh (“Contractor”). The organizations are hereinafter
       individually referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.

       WHEREAS, it is the mutual desire of the parties to amend the Contract in order to modify certain terms and
       conditions thereof, including:

           •    Change the period of performance Completion Date from 31 July 2026 to 13 June 2025 to reflect
                closure of the U.S. Department of Agriculture Bangladesh Trade Facilitation project as of 13 June 2025.
           •    Combine Deliverables 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 to reflect the revised completion date for an operational system
                and handover of source code upon completion of the Contract. Amend Deliverable 9 to reflect that
                the Government of Bangladesh Software Quality Testing Center (SQTC) could not confirm the
                completion and turnaround time for quality testing services. There is no change in the Total Contract
                Value of this Firm-Fixed-Price contract.
           •    Update clause A.7 Payment, in SECTION A, General Terms and Conditions to incorporate revisions to
                the Payment and Deliverables Table as per above points.
           •    Update SECTION C, Scope of Work, Clause IV. Deliverables Table to reflect revisions as per the above
                points.

       IT IS HEREBY AGREED that the Contract between the parties is hereby amended in accordance with the
       provisions set forth below.

       EXCEPT AS HEREIN AMENDED, the terms, conditions, and provisions of the Contract, as amended, shall remain
       in full force and effect.
