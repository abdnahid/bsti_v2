## Slide 2: "VISION: ONE DIGITAL BSTI 2.0" (image)

> *A unified, secure and connected digital ecosystem for a modern, efficient and citizen-centric BSTI*
>
> **The Single-Platform Vision**: To create a cohesive digital infrastructure where every sub-office and inspection wing interfaces natively with centralized governance nodes.

| # | Pillar | Description | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **One National Database** | Consolidated data structures eliminating regional silos. | Single source of truth; real-time data access; consistent information across all offices |
| 02 | **One Unified Platform** | Consistent workflow mechanics for certification, licensing, and testing. | Standardized processes; integrated services; better user experience for citizens and businesses |
| 03 | **One Command Center** | Central security monitoring for all network entry points. | 24/7 system monitoring; proactive threat detection; centralized control and governance |
| 04 | **Unified SLA Framework** | Equal service turnaround speeds from Dhaka head offices to remote border points. | Consistent service standards; time-bound service delivery; transparent performance tracking |

> **Conditions:** (1) No new development for neither existing nor new services. (2) Only issue resolve for live services.
> **Beta Version under New Architecture: 01 Aug, 26 to 31 Dec, 26**

**Why this matters.** The ৳16,25,000 charged for Aug–Dec 2026 (5 services × ৳65,000 × 5 months) is, by OrangeBD's own conditions, **issue resolution only, with no new development**. During the same window, OrangeBD describes the platform as a "beta version under new architecture". BSTI's WP1 treats this period as an O&M lump sum.

> **Discrepancy: MSC Re-certification.** Slide 4 and the headline on slide 31 give **136** features. The module catalogue on slide 31 adds up to **113** (7+10+10+9+10+7+6+9+7+7+4+7+10+10), and so does the SRS table of contents for Service-18. Every other service's headline matches its own catalogue. The correct deck total is therefore **2,145 features**, not 2,168.
> `BSTI 2.0 - Revised Financial Structure.xlsx` (Work Volume row 40, "Deck slide 31") uses **136**. At the variant weight of 0.5, that is 68 work units where it should be 56.5. About 11.5 units (roughly ৳7 lakh at ~৳60,829 per unit) are allocated to WP5 instead of being spread across all packages. The workbook has **not** been changed.
