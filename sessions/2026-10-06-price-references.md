## Key findings

- **Verified maintenance contracts (e-GP awards):**
  - D-Nothi: ৳5.42 cr for 24 months (Dec 2021 – Dec 2023), Tappware Solutions. That is about ৳22.6 lakh/month for ~19,000 offices.
  - e-Mutation (1st gen): ৳1.37 cr, May – Sep 2021, Business Automation Ltd. That is about ৳34.3 lakh/month. It matches OrangeBD's slide 11, so that slide row is verified.
- **The Land systems publish no separate maintenance cost.** Every EOI (LD Tax, DLRMS, e-Mutation 2nd gen, LARMS, LSG) bundles "24x7 support & maintenance as per SLA" into the development contract. What happens after the project ends (June 2026) isn't published.
- **"LAMS" is officially LARMS** (Land Acquisition and Requisition Management System).
- **Vendors:**
  - DLRMS: SoftBD (from its portfolio)
  - e-Mutation 1st gen: Business Automation Ltd
  - LD Tax: Mysoft Heaven, inferred from the app ID and dev domain only
  - BSCIC Online Market: Dream71
  - LSG and LARMS: not found
- **Umbrella project:** Land Management Automation Project, ৳1,197.03 cr, Jul 2020 – Jun 2026, 17 services, 5,247 offices.
- **Not found online:** BCIC PMIS and Fertilizer system (anything), BSCIC ERP value and maintenance, the 27 cr Mouza mapping figure (its parent, the Digital Survey Enhancement Project, is ৳1,212.55 cr).
- **For BSTI:** OrangeBD's proposed maintenance (৳31.35 lakh/month) is above D-Nothi's (national, ~19,000 offices) and in the range of national platforms, for one institution with 23 offices. This is a strong negotiation point.
- **Pattern worth copying:** Land bundles SLA-based support into the development contract, so the build vendor also carries maintenance during the project.
